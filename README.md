# RETAIL-001: Retail Sales Data Analysis

## Project Overview
This project analyzes retail sales data for 2025 to evaluate sales performance, product profitability, category contribution, and monthly trends.

The objective is to identify business opportunities, improve profitability, and support data-driven management decisions.

## Tools & Technologies
- Python
- Pandas and NumPy
- PostgreSQL
- SQL
- Microsoft Power BI
- Jupyter Notebook

## Dataset Overview
- 200 sales transactions
- 10 products
- 4 product categories
- Reporting period: January–December 2025

## Key Performance Indicators

| Metric | Result |
|---|---:|
| Total Sales | 5,377,180 |
| Total Profit | 1,273,940 |
| Total Units Sold | 603 |
| Overall Profit Margin | 23.69% |
| Total Orders | 200 |

## Key Business Findings

1. **Electronics** generated the highest sales (4,358,000) and profit (952,200), with a profit margin of 21.85%.
2. **Accessories** achieved a profit margin of 42.18%.
3. **Stationery** recorded the highest category profit margin at 50%, although its sales contribution was very low.
4. **May** was the strongest month, generating 230,170 in profit.
5. **December** recorded the lowest monthly sales (88,360) and profit (30,180).

## Business Recommendations

- Explore growth opportunities in Accessories and Stationery, while validating customer demand before increasing investment.
- Improve Electronics profitability through cost and pricing reviews.
- Investigate the reasons behind December's low sales and profit.
- Monitor product-level performance and investigate underperforming products before making discontinuation decisions.
- Use monthly performance tracking to identify sales trends and improvement opportunities.

## Project Workflow

1. Data preparation and validation using Python.
2. Business analysis using PostgreSQL and SQL.
3. Dashboard development using Power BI.
4. Executive summary and business recommendations.
